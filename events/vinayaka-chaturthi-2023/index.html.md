# Vinayaka Chaturthi 2023

> Vinayaka Chaturthi 2023 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 15 September 2023. Children came in traditional clothes for Vinayaka Chaturthi on 15 September 2023.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/vinayaka-chaturthi-2023/

[All events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/)

15 September 2023

Children came in traditional clothes for Vinayaka Chaturthi on 15 September 2023.

They brought sweets and offerings from home, placed flowers at the Ganesha altar one by one, and ended the morning with songs and dance in the hall.
