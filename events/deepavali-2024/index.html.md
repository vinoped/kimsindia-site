# Deepavali 2024

> Deepavali 2024 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 29 October 2024. Deepavali at KIMS on 29 October 2024 began at a festival altar decorated with lamps, flowers and sweets.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/deepavali-2024/

29 October 2024

Deepavali at KIMS on 29 October 2024 began at a festival altar decorated with lamps, flowers and sweets.

Children in festive clothes danced with diyas, and the walls carried Deepavali greetings in Tamil and English.
