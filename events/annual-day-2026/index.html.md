# Annual Day 2026: Kaleidoscope

> Annual Day 2026: Kaleidoscope at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 22 March 2026. KIMS and Kudos children took the stage for Kaleidoscope, the Annual Day on 22 March 2026.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/annual-day-2026/

[All events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/)

22 March 2026

KIMS and Kudos children took the stage for Kaleidoscope, the Annual Day on 22 March 2026.

The programme opened with the lighting of the lamp and moved through folk and classical dances, a school band, a Thirukkural recitation in Tamil, a dance about peace and a choir in orange.

It closed with the whole cast on stage together.
