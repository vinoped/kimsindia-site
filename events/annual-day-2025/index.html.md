# Annual Day 2025

> Annual Day 2025 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 23 March 2025. KIMS children took the stage for the Annual Day on 23 March 2025.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/annual-day-2025/

[All events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/)

23 March 2025

KIMS children took the stage for the Annual Day on 23 March 2025.

There were classical and folk dances, a choir, a courtroom play, a student band, and a graduation in caps for the children moving up to primary school.
