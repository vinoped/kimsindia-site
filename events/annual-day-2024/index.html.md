# Annual Day 2024

> Annual Day 2024 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 3 March 2024. The Annual Day on 3 March 2024 filled the stage with colour, in front of a stained-glass KIMS backdrop.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/annual-day-2024/

[All events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/)

3 March 2024

The Annual Day on 3 March 2024 filled the stage with colour, in front of a stained-glass KIMS backdrop.

Children danced to folk, classical and film songs, drummed, sang in their blue school blazers, and the graduating children received their certificates.
