# Karate belt grading 2023

> Karate belt grading 2023 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 21 January 2023. Children in the KIMS karate class were graded on 21 January 2023 and received their new belts and certificates from their instructor.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/karate-2023/

[All events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/)

21 January 2023

Children in the KIMS karate class were graded on 21 January 2023 and received their new belts and certificates from their instructor.

Karate builds focus, balance and confidence. KIMS students have also won trophies at the FCKA 2nd National Level Karate Championship.
