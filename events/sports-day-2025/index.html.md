# Sports Day 2025

> Sports Day 2025 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 15 February 2025. The Sports Day on 15 February 2025 brought KIMS and Kudos children together at Sportify Sports Arena, Medavakkam.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/sports-day-2025/

[All events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/)

15 February 2025 · Sportify Sports Arena

The Sports Day on 15 February 2025 brought KIMS and Kudos children together at Sportify Sports Arena, Medavakkam.

The youngest children ran obstacle races through hoops, tunnels and cones with their teachers beside them, older children performed drills, and the day ended on the podium.
