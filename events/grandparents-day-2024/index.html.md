# Grandparents' Day 2024

> Grandparents' Day 2024 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 26 October 2024. Grandparents were the guests of honour on 26 October 2024.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/grandparents-day-2024/

[All events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/)

26 October 2024

Grandparents were the guests of honour on 26 October 2024.

They filled the courtyard under a festival canopy, and in a little play kitchen the children, in chef's hats, played chef for them.
