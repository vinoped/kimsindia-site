# Christmas 2024

> Christmas 2024 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 18 December 2024. KIMS dressed in red and white for Christmas on 18 December 2024.

18 December 2024

KIMS dressed in red and white for Christmas on 18 December 2024.

Santa visited, children danced and sang in front of the KIMS Christmas set, and each class had its photo taken with him.
