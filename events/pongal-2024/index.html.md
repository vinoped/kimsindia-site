> Pongal 2024 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 10 January 2024. KIMS celebrated Pongal on 10 January 2024 with a village set: a decorated hut, sugarcane, cows and a Pongal pot.

10 January 2024

KIMS celebrated Pongal on 10 January 2024 with a village set: a decorated hut, sugarcane, cows and a Pongal pot.

Children came in traditional clothes, drew kolam, danced on the turf and watched a potter shape clay pots.
