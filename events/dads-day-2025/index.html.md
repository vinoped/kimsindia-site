# Dad's Day 2025

> Dad's Day 2025 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 25 January 2025. Fathers came to school on 25 January 2025 for Dad's Day.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/dads-day-2025/

[All events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/)

25 January 2025

Fathers came to school on 25 January 2025 for Dad's Day.

They played games and danced with their children in the playground, and posed with them on a classic motorbike.
