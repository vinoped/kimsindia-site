## Memorised answers won't protect your child's future. Understanding will.

AI tools can already recite every answer in the textbook. The children who do well will be the ones who understand ideas well enough to use them.

- Stuck when the question is asked differently
- Competes with AI at what AI does best

- Applies it to new problems with confidence

- Speak fluently in English, and read and write sentences on their own

- Hold a simple conversation in Tamil and in Hindi
