# Independence Day 2024

> Independence Day 2024 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 10 August 2024. KIMS and Kudos celebrated Independence Day on 10 August 2024 with the theme Unity in Diversity.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/independence-day-2024/

[All events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/)

10 August 2024

KIMS and Kudos celebrated Independence Day on 10 August 2024 with the theme Unity in Diversity.

Children came dressed as soldiers, freedom fighters and people from different states, sang and danced in the colours of the flag, and families watched from the hall.
