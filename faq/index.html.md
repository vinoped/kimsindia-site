# KIMS, in plain answers

> Answers about KIMS Nursery and Primary School (Kudos Indian Montessori Shikshalaya), Selaiyur, Chennai: classes, Montessori method, recognition, admissions, location and contact.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/faq/

Everything a parent usually asks before a first visit. Still curious? Call **98842 61592** or **739 739 4336**.

### What is KIMS?

KIMS Nursery and Primary School, also known as **Kudos Indian Montessori Shikshalaya**, is a Montessori school in Selaiyur, Chennai, for children from Pre-KG to Class 5. It is recognized by the Tamil Nadu Government and is an initiative of The VEDIC Educational and Charitable Trust.

### Where is KIMS located?

KIMS, Avvai Nagar, Indira Nagar West Extension, Selaiyur, Chennai 600073. It is close to Camp Road, Rajakilpakkam, Madambakkam, Sembakkam, Chitlapakkam and East Tambaram. [Get directions](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pvtjJJ6JbX8Po89R7).

### Which classes does KIMS offer?

Pre-KG, LKG and UKG in Montessori early-years classrooms, and Classes 1 to 5, which follow the Tamil Nadu State Board syllabus. Classes 6 to 12 are planned for children who come up through KIMS. For Pre-KG, your child should have completed 3 years by 31 March of the year they join.

### What are the school hours?

Monday to Friday, and some Saturdays. Pre-KG: 9:30 am to 12:30 pm. LKG and UKG: 9:30 am to 2:30 pm. Classes 1 to 5: 9:30 am to 3 pm.

### How does KIMS teach?

Through the Montessori method: children learn each idea with hands-on materials before moving to the abstract, at their own pace. We build [ten learning superpowers](https://www.kimsindia.com/philosophy/), from curiosity and clear reasoning to kindness and a growth mindset, so children understand rather than memorise.

### What are the 10 Superpowers of Learning?

The educational philosophy of Dr Sindu Sukumaran, D.Litt, Principal and Correspondent of KIMS: The Explorer, The Detective, The Builder, The Maker, The Chameleon, The Teacher, The Superhero, The Time Traveler, The Shapeshifter and The Upgrader. Each is a real part of human intelligence that machines find hardest to copy. [See all ten](https://www.kimsindia.com/philosophy/#superpowers).

### Is KIMS recognized by the government?

Yes. KIMS Nursery and Primary School is recognized by the Tamil Nadu Government.

### Which languages do children learn?

English, with Tamil and Hindi. By the end of UKG, children aim to speak fluently in English and hold a simple conversation in Tamil and in Hindi.

### How do I apply for admission?

Fill in the [admission enquiry form](https://www.kimsindia.com/admission-enquiry/), or call or WhatsApp 98842 61592 or 739 739 4336. We arrange a visit to see a class, a short meeting with the teachers, and then confirm the admission.

### Can I visit the school first?

Yes, please do. Watching a class at work is the best way to judge a school. Call or WhatsApp 98842 61592 or 739 739 4336 to fix a time.

### What events and activities happen at KIMS?

An Annual Day on stage, a Sports Day, Grandparents', Mom's and Dad's Days, festivals such as Pongal, Deepavali, Christmas, Vinayaka Chaturthi and Onam, Independence Day, colour days for the youngest children, field trips, and karate, where KIMS students won trophies at the FCKA 2nd National Karate Championship. Children also take part in the Science Olympiad Foundation's international Olympiads in science, maths, English, general knowledge, computers, social studies and Hindi. [See our events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/).
