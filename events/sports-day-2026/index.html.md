# Sports Day 2026

> Sports Day 2026 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 1 March 2026. KIMS and Kudos held their Annual Sports Meet on 1 March 2026 at Sportify Sports Arena, Medavakkam.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/sports-day-2026/

[All events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/)

1 March 2026 · Sportify Sports Arena

KIMS and Kudos held their Annual Sports Meet on 1 March 2026 at Sportify Sports Arena, Medavakkam.

It began with the march past, class by class, then drills with ribbons, rings and discs, races and obstacle games for every age, and ended with medals, trophies and the podium.
