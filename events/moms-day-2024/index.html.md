# Mom's Day 2024

> Mom's Day 2024 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 14 December 2024. On 14 December 2024 mothers spent the morning at school with their children.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/moms-day-2024/

[All events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/)

14 December 2024

On 14 December 2024 mothers spent the morning at school with their children.

There were games and dancing in the playground, a moon-and-stars photo corner, and time on the climbing frame and slides.
