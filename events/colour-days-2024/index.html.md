# Red and Yellow Days 2024

> Red and Yellow Days 2024 at KIMS Nursery and Primary School, Selaiyur, Chennai, on 4 November 2024. Colour days help the youngest children learn colours by living them for a day.

Source: https://www.kimsindia.com/events/colour-days-2024/

[All events](https://www.kimsindia.com/events/)

4 November 2024

Colour days help the youngest children learn colours by living them for a day.

On 4 and 5 November 2024 they came dressed in red and then in yellow, played with balloons in rooms decorated in each colour, and took photos in the red and yellow corners.
